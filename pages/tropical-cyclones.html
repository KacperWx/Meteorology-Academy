Tropical Meteorology

University-level tropical meteorology focusing on the physical processes governing tropical cyclone formation, evolution and prediction.

Tropical Cyclones

This course develops the dynamical and thermodynamic foundations required to understand tropical cyclones, progressing from atmospheric structure through to advanced vortex dynamics and forecasting.

Level

University

Duration

≈ 15 hours

Lessons

8

Module 1 — Fundamentals

1. Tropical Atmospheric Structure

Vertical thermal structure, lapse rates, tropical tropopause, stability and atmospheric layers.

2. Hydrostatic Balance

Derivation of the hydrostatic equation and its application to large-scale tropical circulations.

3. Geopotential Height

Pressure surfaces, atmospheric thickness and interpretation of upper-air analyses.

Module 2 — Forces and Balances

4. Pressure Gradient Force

Mathematical formulation, pressure coordinates, and atmospheric acceleration.

5. Coriolis Force in the Tropics

Latitude dependence, β-effect and limitations close to the equator.

6. Gradient Wind Balance

Gradient wind equation, cyclonic and anticyclonic flow, and force balance.

Module 3 — Dynamical Diagnostics

7. Rossby Number

Relative importance of inertial and Coriolis forces across atmospheric scales.

8. Potential Vorticity

Conservation of potential vorticity and its role in tropical cyclone dynamics.
