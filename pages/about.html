About Meteorology Academy

We teach the physics behind the forecast — from the balance of forces aloft to the structure of the storms that form beneath them.

00 — Mission

Why this exists

Meteorology Academy was built to make the theory behind weather forecasting accessible without watering it down. Each lesson works through the same reasoning a working meteorologist relies on — balanced forces, conserved quantities, and the literature that underpins them — one concept at a time.

01 — What You'll Learn

Course pillars

●

Atmospheric Dynamics

The forces that shape wind: pressure gradients, Coriolis effects, and the balances between them.

●

Tropical Meteorology

Formation, structure and evolution of tropical cyclones, grounded in vortex dynamics.

●

Applied Literature

Reading and interpreting the research that established the frameworks used today.
