University-Level Meteorology Education

Learn the science behind every forecast.

We teach the physics behind the forecast—from atmospheric dynamics and tropical cyclone theory to the research literature that underpins modern meteorology.

Our Mission

Bridging theory and forecasting

Meteorology Academy was built to make the theory behind weather forecasting accessible without watering it down. Each lesson follows the same reasoning a professional meteorologist relies on—from balanced forces and conserved quantities to the research literature that established modern forecasting methods.

What We Teach

Core Academic Pillars

PILLAR 01

Tropical Meteorology

Formation, structure and evolution of tropical cyclones, grounded in vortex dynamics.

PILLAR 02

Atmospheric Dynamics

Governing equations of motion, Navier-Stokes, and balanced flow approximations.

PILLAR 03

Synoptic Analysis

Interpretation of real-time weather charts, frontogenesis, and mid-latitude systems.
