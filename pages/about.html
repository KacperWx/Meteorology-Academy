Our Mission

Bridging theory and forecasting

Meteorology Academy was built to make the theory behind weather forecasting accessible without watering it down. Each lesson follows the same reasoning a professional meteorologist relies on—from balanced forces and conserved quantities to the research literature that established modern forecasting methods.
