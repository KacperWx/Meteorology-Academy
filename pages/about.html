Learn the science behind every forecast.

Our Mission

Bridging theory and forecasting

Meteorology Academy was built to make the theory behind weather forecasting accessible without watering it down.

Each lesson follows the same reasoning a professional meteorologist relies on—from balanced forces and conserved quantities to the research literature that established modern forecasting methods.

What We Teach

Core Academic Pillars

01 / Dynamics

Tropical Meteorology

Formation, structure and evolution of tropical cyclones, grounded in vortex dynamics.

02 / Physics

Atmospheric Motion

Governing equations of fluid dynamics, thermodynamic profiles, and balanced force approximations.

03 / Synthesis

Synoptic Analysis

Interpretation of numerical weather prediction models, frontogenesis, and observational charts.
